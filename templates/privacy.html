Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon use

At Spendly, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our personal expense tracking service, how we use it, and the steps we take to keep it safe. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1

Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the Service. This falls into two categories:

Account information — provided when you register:

  • Username used to identify your account.
  • Password stored as a secure hash — we never store plain-text passwords.

Expense data — entered by you while using the app:

  • Amount the value of each expense.
  • Category e.g. Food, Bills, Transport.
  • Date when the expense occurred.
  • Description an optional note you add.

We do not collect payment card details, bank account numbers, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2

How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • Provide the Service — authenticate your account and display your personal expense history.
  • Generate insights — calculate category totals, monthly summaries, and spending trends shown within the app.
  • Maintain security — detect and prevent unauthorised access or abuse.
  • Improve the app — understand general usage patterns (never tied to individual identities) to make Spendly better.

We will never use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that affects your rights.

3

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server or device where Spendly is running. We apply the following safeguards:

  • Password hashing: All passwords are hashed using a modern one-way algorithm before storage.
  • Session management: User sessions are managed securely with expiry controls.
  • Access controls: Only you can view or modify your own expense records.
  • No cloud sync: Data is not transmitted to external cloud services unless explicitly configured by the host.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of data storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention: Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion at any time (see Contact Us below).

4

Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services solely to deliver the user interface:

  • Google Fonts — fonts (DM Serif Display, DM Sans) are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. Google's standard font-serving logs may apply. No personal data from your Spendly account is shared with Google.

We do not integrate analytics platforms (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising networks, social media trackers, or any other third-party data brokers.

If third-party integrations are added in the future, this policy will be updated and users notified in advance.

5

Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted.
  • Portability: Request your expense data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to any processing of your data that you believe is unlawful.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below. We will respond within 30 days.

6

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Significant changes will be communicated via an in-app notice or email where reasonably practicable. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
